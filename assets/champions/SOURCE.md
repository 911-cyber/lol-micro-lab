# Champion references

Riot Data Dragon 16.20.1 ja_JP champion.json supplies names and base attack range/speed/damage/movement. Portraits: /cdn/16.20.1/img/champion/{id}.png. Loading artwork: /cdn/img/champion/loading/{id}_0.jpg.

https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol#data-dragon

The in-game models are procedural simplified interpretations, not official Riot models. The attack trainer implements basic attacks with training-normalized HP, not full live-game kits, items, armor or level scaling.
